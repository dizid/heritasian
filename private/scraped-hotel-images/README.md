# Scraped hotel photos (not published)

These images were downloaded from the hotels' own websites by the deprecated
`scripts/_deprecated/fetch-hotel-images.js` (no licence, no attribution; see
`scripts/_deprecated/README.md`). On Nov 12 2026 (Chief decision T-0532) they
were taken off the live site: every hotel now shows the generic site image
(`GENERIC_HOTEL_IMAGE` in `src/data/db.ts`). Kept here, out of `public/`, so
nothing is deleted. Do not move them back into `public/`; use licensed photos
(e.g. Wikimedia Commons with attribution) instead.
